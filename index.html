Who We Train

Most people on the mat started with no experience. Classes are split so a first day has a clear place to stand.

01

Kids, 5–12

After-school classes for focus, coordination, and respect. Children learn how to try, miss, and come back calm.

02

Teens, 13–17

A structured room for teenagers who want to train hard. Jiu-jitsu, fitness, and coaching that treats them as athletes.

03

Adults, from zero

People who live or work in Tung Chung and want training without a trip to Central. Fundamentals classes assume you have never tied a belt.

04

Practical self-defense

Short classes in normal workout clothes. Distance, balance, and a few escapes you can remember under stress.
