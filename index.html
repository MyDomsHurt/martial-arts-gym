Common Questions

I’m a complete beginner. Is that okay?

Yes. Most people start with no experience. Coaches will place you and guide you through the first class.

What should I bring?

Comfortable clothes you can move in and water. For boxing we can lend gloves if needed. For open mat, athletic clothes are fine.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

No. Your first class is free. After that you can choose month-to-month or drop-in. No long-term commitment required to start.

Where are you in Yau Tong?

Short walk from Yau Tong Station. Exact address and directions are on the contact page.
