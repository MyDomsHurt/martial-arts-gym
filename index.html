Common Questions

I’m a complete beginner. Is that okay?

Yes. The coach places you and walks you through the first class.

What should I bring?

Clothes you can move in, and water. We can lend what you need for that visit.

Is there a monthly contract?

No. Your first class is free. After that you buy credits. They do not expire, and you can book any class up to 14 days ahead.

Where are you in Yau Tong?

Short walk from Yau Tong Station. Exact address and directions are on the contact page.
