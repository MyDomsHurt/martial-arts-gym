Weekly frequency

Boxing & Martial Arts Fitness

The planned rate for these classes. Once we open, count sessions inside a rolling 7 days. The first session in that window is 26 credits. Each further session in the same 7 days costs less, down to 13 credits from the fifth onward.

  1. 1st26credits
  2. 2nd19credits
  3. 3rd16credits
  4. 4th14credits
  5. 5th+13credits each

Example

Three Boxing sessions in the same 7 days: 26 + 19 + 16 = 61 credits. Three sessions at the first-session rate would be 78. Coming back twice in that window saves 17 credits.

Consecutive hours

Open Mat

Unstructured community training. Find a partner and roll. No coach leading.

The planned rate for one visit. Once we open, hours in a row cost less than the same hours taken separately. The first hour is 10 credits. The second consecutive hour adds 6. The third adds 4.

Open mat credits for one, two, and three consecutive hours
1 hour 2 hours 3 hours
10 credits 16 credits 20 credits

Example

Two separate 1-hour visits are 10 + 10 = 20 credits. One 3-hour visit is also 20 credits. Three hours in a row cost the same as two single hours.
