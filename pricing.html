What the trial includes

One class in the program you pick, with a coach who knows it is your first day. Wear a t-shirt and shorts you can move in. We lend gloves for boxing, or a rashguard for open mat, if you need them that visit.

How plans work

No joining fee. Plans renew each month until you cancel. Cancel or pause with 14 days’ notice by email. We confirm the figure again when you book, before any payment.

What is not included

Private lessons are quoted on their own. After the trial, bring your own gloves for boxing and a rashguard for open mat. Fitness needs training shoes and a t-shirt.
