Ages 5–12

Kids Martial Arts

A first martial art, built for children. Classes mix movement games with simple striking and grappling so kids learn to listen, fall safely, and try again.

  • Two groups: ages 5–8 and 9–12
  • Parents may watch from the bench
  • Focus, coordination, and respect come before trophies

45–60 minutes · After school and Saturday morning

Ages 13–17

Teens

Training that respects how strong teens already are. Brazilian jiu-jitsu fundamentals, footwork, and conditioning, with a room that stays disciplined.

  • School stress stays off the mat
  • Coaches who speak to teenagers directly
  • A path into adult classes when they are ready

60 minutes · Weekday late afternoon and Friday

Ages 18+

Adult BJJ

Brazilian jiu-jitsu from the first class onward. You learn how to close distance, stay calm under pressure, and finish a round without getting hurt.

  • Fundamentals classes assume no experience
  • All-levels rounds paired by size when we can
  • Gi and no-gi positions, taught plainly

75 minutes · Weekday evenings and Saturday

Ages 16+

Muay Thai

Thai boxing for fitness and skill. Pads, bag work, clinch, and footwork. Beginners do not spar. Sparring is a separate class, and only after a coach clears you.

  • Every level starts on mechanics
  • Gloves and shin guards lent for the trial
  • Wednesday sparring is invite-only

60 minutes · Weekday evenings and Saturday

Ages 16+

Self-Defense

Short, practical sessions for everyday situations. Voice, distance, balance, and a few escapes you can use in normal clothes. No experience needed.

  • Tuesday lunchtime fits a work break
  • Wednesday evening is the longer class
  • No macho talk, and no surprise sparring

45–75 minutes · Tuesday and Wednesday
