Instructors

Coaches on the floor.

Four coaches. Each one teaches the classes on the schedule under their name. Your trial is led by the coach of that class.

Train With a Coach
EL

Evan Lau

Head Coach · Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu

Black belt. Evan has coached adults and teens for 15 years, including beginners who now train three nights a week. Classes are technical and calm. He wants a clean position before a scramble.

  • BJJ black belt
  • Adult BJJ and the teen classes
  • 15 years of coaching
PS

Priya Santos

Muay Thai Coach

Priya trained in Bangkok for two years and has coached in Hong Kong since. Sessions start with footwork and pads. Beginners stay on drills. She decides who is ready for the Wednesday sparring class.

  • Muay Thai coach
  • All-levels striking and clinch
  • Sparring by invitation only
DC

Daniel Cho

Kids Program Lead

Daniel is a former primary-school PE teacher. He runs the kids classes like a good classroom: short instructions, lots of turns, and a clear close to every hour. Ages 5–8 and 9–12 train apart.

  • Kids program, ages 5–12
  • Former primary-school PE teacher
  • Saturday mixed-age hour
HI

Hana Iqbal

Self-Defense Coach

Hana comes from judo and from teaching practical self-defense to adults who do not want a combat sport. Tuesday lunchtime is 45 minutes in workout clothes. Wednesday evening adds simple grappling escapes.

  • Self-defense and judo background
  • Lunchtime and Wednesday evening
  • Also coaches Wednesday teens
