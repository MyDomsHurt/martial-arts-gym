Book a Free Trial

What the trial includes

One class in the program you pick, with a coach who knows it is your first day. It costs nothing and uses no credits. Wear a t-shirt and shorts you can move in. We lend gloves for boxing, or a rashguard for open mat, if you need them that visit.

How credits work

Buy a pack at the desk after your trial. Book any class up to 14 days ahead. Open mat is 10 credits for one hour, 16 for two hours back to back, and 20 for three. Boxing and martial arts fitness share one count in a rolling 7 days: 23 credits, then 19, 16, 14, and 13 from the fifth session on. Open mat does not count toward that week. No joining fee and no monthly renewal. Unused credits stay on your account. We confirm the number again when you book, before any payment.

What is not included

Private lessons are quoted on their own. After the trial, bring your own gloves for boxing and a rashguard for open mat. Fitness needs training shoes and a t-shirt.
